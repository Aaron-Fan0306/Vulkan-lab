A shader gives the mathematical calculation to where to draw the pixel/texel to screen.(calculates position)
A rendering command gives instruction to draw it
A vkImage is NOT a rendered image, its a blank canva/paint palet for shaders to calculate the pixels and draw on it

Rendering commands = restaurant order
Shaders            = recipe
GPU                = cooks
VkImage            = plate

A physical device is the supported feature the user's hardware can support, varies depending on the gpu(AMD, NVIDIA, Intel, etc)
A logical device is the usable connection to a selected physical device. Request extensions, queues and features.

A VkSurfacKHR connects vulkan presentation to an OS window, where the image will be draw to.

A swapchain, a queue of vkimage wait to be drawn, creates the format and size and type of vk image you want on creation
